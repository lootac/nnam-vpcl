NNAM Volunteer Pocket Checklist

Built from the NNAM Volunteer Guide (revised 20 March 2025) as a study aid. Not an official Museum publication.

Using this checklist

A pocket checklist in the Navy style. The white side covers your watch, each volunteer division, and reference facts. Flip the book over, or tap EMER, for the yellow emergency side. Tap a step to check it off. Dotted words explain themselves: point at them, or press and hold on a phone.

    Touch screen: swipe up on the page for the next page, down for the previous page. Swipe left to go back to the page you viewed before, right to go forward again, like a web browser. With a mouse, use ↶ Back and Fwd ↷.
