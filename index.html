NNAM Volunteer Pocket Checklist

Built from the NNAM Volunteer Guide (revised 20 March 2025) as a study aid. Not an official Museum publication.

Using this checklist

A pocket checklist in the Navy style. The white side covers your watch, each volunteer division, and reference facts. Flip the book over, or tap EMER, for the yellow emergency side. Tap a step to check it off. Tap any dotted word for a plain-English explanation. Tap a boxed page number to go there. Tap anywhere else on a line to check it off.

    Touch screen: swipe up on the page for the next page, down for the previous page. Swipe left to go back to the page you viewed before, right to go forward again, like a web browser. Every swipe also has a button in the bar under the book, so nothing depends on gestures.
    turn pages · emergency index · flip the book
    Tap a key here, or press it on your keyboard.
    Install it like an app (works offline once opened): on iPhone or iPad, tap Safari's Share button, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, Windows or Mac with Chrome or Edge, choose Install from the browser menu, or tap More → Install as an app when it appears.

    Unofficial study aid prepared by a volunteer. The NNAM Volunteer Guide and Museum staff direction govern wherever they differ from this checklist. Where the Guide's own sections disagree, the step is marked † and the explanation says so.
